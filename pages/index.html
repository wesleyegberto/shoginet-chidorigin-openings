About this translation

This is an English translation of Chidorigin's Opening Encyclopedia (戦法図鑑), a comprehensive strategy guide covering over 220 chapters of Japanese Shogi openings, castles, and tactics.

To preserve technical accuracy and readability for the international Shogi community, the translation adheres to specific conventions:

  • Original Japanese Notation: Moves (e.g., ▲７六歩, ▽３四歩) and coordinates (e.g., 3三, 2六) are kept in their original Japanese format.
  • Sente and Gote: The terms Sente (first player) and Gote (second player) are preserved directly.
  • Tactic Names: Japanese tactics, castles, and opening names are formatted as Romaji (English Name, Kanji) (e.g., Yagura (Yagura Castle, 矢倉)).
