Jumping with ▽２五桂 to take the Pawn on Sente's Rook file and sacrificing the Knight is the core tactic of this Demon Killer Opposing Rook.

After Sente takes with ▲同飛, Gote counterattacks along the Rook file starting with ▽２四歩. When Gote plays ▽２六歩, Sente desperately defends with ▲３九桂, but in truth, Sente's position is already crushed. ▽４九角 is the final decisive move; Sente's ▲１六角 aims to meet ▽３八角成 with ▲同角 to hold on, but Gote pushes the Bishop back with ▽１五歩, leading to a winning position for Gote up to Figure 2. Having pushed the edge Pawn with ▽１四歩 earlier was specifically for this variation.

After ▽２五桂 is taken with ▲同飛, no matter how the game branches, Sente has no means to prevent Gote's counterattack along the Rook file. Therefore, declining the sacrificed Knight is a potential consideration.

If Sente does not take ▽２五桂, there is no immediate concern of having the Rook file breached. Sente waits while solidifying their King, but Gote's second deadly weapon in this strategy is targeting the edge starting with ▽１五歩. From ▽１七桂成 down to dropping ▽２五香 in Figure 3, Sente is once again left in trouble due to a lack of Pawns. There is no way it could be good to unconditionally give up a Pawn on the Rook file in the early game. Consequently, the moment ▽２五桂 is played, Sente is already at a disadvantage.

All of this came about because playing ▲２六歩 followed by ▲２五歩 made Gote's Opposing Rook (Mukaibisha, 向かい飛車) possible. At this point, one might wonder if starting with ▲７六歩 to open the Bishop's diagonal on the first move would fix this. That development will be covered in the next chapter.
