When Gote drops ▽８九飛, Sente's sacrifice move ▲５三歩成 is crucial. Neglecting this allows Gote to hit back hard with ▽５五香; however, by sacrificing the Pawn first, Sente ensures a future Lance drop can be easily blunted with a simple Pawn defense. After Gote responds with ▽同歩, Sente drops ▲８八歩 to reach Figure 4. Left unanswered, this Pawn sets up a decisive ▲７三桂不成 (translation note: the original text says "▽７三桂不成" but seems to be a typo) jump for Sente. If Gote takes with ▽同飛, Sente responds with ▲５五角; if Gote captures with ▽同馬, Sente still plays ▲７三桂不成 to solidify an overwhelmingly winning position. The key lies in promoting to "non-promoted" (Funanari) Knight status, targeting ▲６一桂成 on the very next turn. A Pawn dropped right at the intersection of multiple enemy attack lines—like ▲８八歩 in Figure 4—is known as a Focus Pawn (Shōten no Fu, 焦点の歩), an essential tactical Pawn technique well worth mastering.
