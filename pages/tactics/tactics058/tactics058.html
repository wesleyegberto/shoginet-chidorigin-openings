Sente's Aggressive Opposing Rook (Mukaibisha, 先手の攻める向かい飛車) [1]

Up until the previous chapter, we explained Opposing Rook (Mukaibisha, 向かい飛車) when playing as Gote, but the principles can also be applied when playing as Sente, and being a move ahead can sometimes be an advantage. However, when Sente adopts Opposing Rook, a special Sente-only move sequence can be used with the primary focus on attacking right from the start.

Sente playing ▲５六歩 on move 3 is an opening strategy in its own right. Against move 4 ▽８五歩, if Gote plays ▽５四歩, Sente transposes to Central Rook (Nakabisha, 中飛車) with ▲５八飛; if Gote plays ▽３四歩, Sente transposes to Nakabisha with ▲５五歩 followed by ▲５八飛. In this way, playing ▲５六歩 after ▲７六歩 allows Sente to alternate between Nakabisha and Opposing Rook depending on Gote's response.

However, if Sente plays ▲５六歩 when move 2 is ▽３四歩, Gote can exchange Bishops and create a Horse with ▽５七角, so this strategy is limited to when Gote's 2nd move is ▽８四歩.

In response to Gote reinforcing the ５三 square with ▽６二銀, Sente pushes for a Rook-file Pawn exchange with ▲８六歩. After ▲８六同角, Sente threatens a direct Rook capture starting with ▲４二角成 similar to the tactic explained in Chapter 50, so Gote defends against this with ▽８五歩, forcing Sente's Bishop back to ▲７七角 to reach Figure 1.

Since the ５三 square is protected by ▽６二銀, opening the Bishop's diagonal here with ▽３四歩 is a possible consideration, but against this Sente has the tactical line of playing ▲２二角成 followed by ▲８四歩. Taking with ▽同飛 allows Sente's ▲６六角, a double threat targeting both Rook and Silver, so Gote instead plays ▽９五角 to capture the Pawn on ８四 with check. However, following ▲７七角, the game proceeds along a sequence with almost no room for variation. In Figure 2, the presence of Sente's Tokin is significant; furthermore, even if Gote drops a Rook with ▽８九飛, Sente responds with ▲７九金, and no matter where Gote's Rook promotes, Sente has counter-drops such as ▲６六角 or ▲７七角 that leave Gote at a disadvantage. Thus, Gote has no openings for a Rook drop, resulting in a position with Sente holding the advantage.

Since the 5th file Pawn has been advanced with ▽５四歩, Gote cannot immediately play ▽４二玉. Gote plays ▽６四歩 before playing ▽４二玉, but Sente also moves their King with ▲４八玉, and moving up with ▲６六角 is the brilliant move that secures the advantage.

This move aims for ▲８四歩 followed by ▲８五飛, which is surprisingly difficult to defend against. If Gote plays ▽６五歩, Sente responds with the check ▲７五角, and below, ▽３二玉 ▲８四歩 ▽７四歩 ▲３一角成 ▽同角 ▲８三銀 results in a winning position for Sente. Therefore, Gote plays ▽７四歩 followed by ▽７三桂 before playing ▽６五歩, but down to Figure 3, it still results in a winning position for Sente.
