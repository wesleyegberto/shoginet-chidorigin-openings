To avoid the sequence from the previous chapter, Sente also advances their pieces with caution. After Gote completes the basic skeleton of Opposing Rook (Mukaibisha) up to ▽４三銀, Gote moves the left Gold up with ▽３二金 on the attacking side. Although the defense around Gote's King becomes weaker, this layout enables a strong offensive stance. Then, while the King is still sitting in its starting position (Igyoku), Gote strikes early with ▽２四歩. Since a Rook exchange is unfavorable for Static Rook, Sente declines it with ▲２五歩, leading to Figure 1 below.

The ▽３二金 variation of Opposing Rook is extremely resilient and can strongly force a Rook/Bishop exchange. Hand-passing the edge file after ▲９六歩 to quickly consolidate shape with ▽７二玉, and then aiming for a Bishop exchange with ▽４五歩, constitutes the second wave of Gote's offensive strategy.

After ▲３三角成 ▽同桂, Gote aims for ▽２五飛 on the next move. If Sente declines the Bishop exchange with ▲６六歩 instead of ▲３三角成, Gote proceeds with ▽４四角 followed by ▽３三桂, continuing to target Sente's Pawn on ２五. Allowing Gote to play ▽２五飛 makes a Rook exchange unavoidable, putting Sente at a disadvantage; therefore, Sente defends with ▲３七桂. However, through the sequence leading to Figure 3, Gote succeeds in creating a Horse (Uma), achieving a comfortable and favorable position.
