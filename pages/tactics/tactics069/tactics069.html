If Gote refrains from taking this Pawn and instead defends with ▽２二銀, Sente responds with ▲２三歩成 ▽同銀 ▲２四歩打, and regardless of where the Silver retreats, Sente's decisive strike with ▲２三角打 completely crushes Gote's defense. Gote is forced to capture with ▽同歩, but if Gote tries to defend with ▽２三歩打 after ▲同飛, Sente plays ▲３四飛, which is a simultaneous check and attack on the Horse—game over. Thus, Gote defends with ▽４四馬 following ▲同飛; however, Sente's ▲２三角打 leads straight to Figure 2, securing a winning position. If Gote runs to ▽４二玉 in response to ▲２三角打, Sente wins with ▲４一角成 followed by ▲２一飛成.

Next is the variation where Gote defends with ▽４四歩 following ▲同飛. This move prevents Sente's ▲３四飞 (which threatens check and horse capture) while simultaneously protecting the Knight on ２一; it also removes the aforementioned line involving ▲４一飛成, allowing Gote's King to escape with ▽４二玉. However, after ▲３四角成 followed by the Pawn drop ▲２二歩 in Figure 3, Sente still holds a winning advantage. As shown above, from Figure 1 onward, every single variation leads to a victory for Sente. This technique of shifting the attack from the 5th file over to the left flank is exceptionally potent and frequently appears in other opening variations as well, so we will revisit and analyze it in those future chapters.
