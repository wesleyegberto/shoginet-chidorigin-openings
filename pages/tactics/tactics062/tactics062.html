Floating/Buoyant Rook Feint Strategy (Ukibisha me kuramashi Senpō, 浮き飛車目くらまし戦法)

From the opening sequence where Sente settles the position early with ▲２六歩 followed by ▲２五歩, there is a rather peculiar tactic with a unique objective. In this chapter, we will introduce this strategy along with its proper response.

After ▲２五歩 and ▽３三角, Sente plays ▲２六飛 on move 5 to form a floating Rook. The goal of this ▲２六飛 is to move to ▲３六飛 next, aiming directly at capturing Gote's undefended Pawn on ３四. Gote's ▽３二飛 seems like a completely natural response, intending to meet ▲３六飛 with ▽２二角. However, through the unavoidable sequence leading up to Figure 1, Gote suddenly finds that the ３四 Pawn can no longer be defended—leaving Gote bewildered, as if tricked by a fox. It is a truly mysterious strategy. Still, there is no reason Gote should accept an uncompensated Pawn loss so early in the opening. Let us examine a different way to respond.

Against ▲２六飛, Gote pushes ▽８四歩, and even after ▲３六飛, continues pushing with ▽８五歩. The idea is that if Sente takes the Pawn with ▲３四飛, Gote counters immediately with ▽８六歩. Since taking the Pawn would indeed be bad, Sente plays ▲９六歩 instead. Gote then defends with ▽８四飛, which appears to solve the problem safely on the surface, but Sente maneuvers with ▲７五角 followed by ▲６六角 to clear the Bishop. To prevent Sente's ▲８三角 after the Bishop exchange, Gote plays ▽７二金. However, upon reaching Figure 2 with Sente floating the Rook to ▲６五飛, Gote cannot stop Sente's next move, ▲８五飛. Trying to defend with ▽８四飛 fails to ▲６六角. While there are other variations along the way, none are promising, and none serve as a decisive way to punish ▲２六飛.

So, what is the best way to play? If you have been following along up to the previous chapter, you might start to realize why this strategy was introduced here.

Exactly! The ultimate refutation to punish this strategy is switching to Opposing Rook (Mukaibisha, 向かい飛車) with ▽２二飛, as explained in the previous chapters. Thinking about it calmly, Sente's floating Rook has moved closer on its own, making it a dream target. Down to Figure 3, Sente captures the Pawn on ３四 as planned, but Gote retaliates by reclaiming the Pawn with ▽２五歩. This exchange clearly favors Gote due to the impending counterattack along the 2nd file. If Sente captures with ▲同歩 in response to Gote's ▽２四歩, Gote plays ▽同飛; Sente then fails to capture the ３四 Pawn and is forced to play the defensive ▲２八歩, resulting in a disadvantage for Sente.

Even if Sente chooses not to play ▲３六飛 and instead proceeds with piece development, the position of ▲２六飛 remains exceptionally dangerous when facing Mukaibisha—a fact that should be clear to anyone who followed the earlier chapters.
