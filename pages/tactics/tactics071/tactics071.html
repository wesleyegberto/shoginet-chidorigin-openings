Flounder/Flatfish Strategy (ヒラメ戦法) [1]

A key characteristic of the Hirame Senpō is that after swinging the Rook on the very first move with ▲５八飛, Sente responds to Gote's ▽３四歩 with ▲６八銀 without closing the Bishop's diagonal. Aside from this, Sente castles the King into a Mino formation and proceeds with piece development just like a standard Central Rook setup.

When the Bishop exchange occurs, standard play calls for recapturing with ▲同銀 to prevent the opponent's Rook Pawn exchange. However, in the Hirame Senpō, Sente recaptures with ▲同桂 instead. The moment Gote pushes ▽８六歩 to exchange Pawns along the 8th file, Sente strikes with ▲５五歩, exchanging the 5th file Pawn to clear the path and activate the Rook. Sente then meets Gote's promotion move ▽８九飛成 with ▲５九金左, completing the piece setup. This low, flat defensive posture earned the strategy its name due to its resemblance to a flatfish clinging to the ocean floor. The sequence leading to Figure 2 represents one potential variation, where the secret to success lies in light, flexible piece maneuvers.
