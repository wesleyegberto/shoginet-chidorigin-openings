Demon Killer Opposing Rook (Oni-goroshi Mukaibisha, 鬼殺し向かい飛車) [1]

Similar to Sakata Style (Sakata-ryu, 阪田流), rather than standard joseki-style Ranging Rook openings that close the Bishop's diagonal, this chapter and the next will introduce Demon Killer Opposing Rook (Onikoroshi Mukaibisha, 鬼殺し向かい飛車), an off-book play (rikisen, 力戦) Opposing Rook that contains aims similar to the Onikoroshi (Demon Killer, 鬼殺し) explained in Chapters 20 and 21.

Upon playing ▲２五歩 ▽３三角 and then ▲７六歩 to open the Bishop's diagonal, Gote immediately ranges the Rook with ▽２二飛 without closing the Bishop's diagonal, followed by ▲３三角成 ▽同桂 to reach Figure 1, which is the basic formation of Demon Killer Opposing Rook. In this position, the move ▲６五角 dropping a Bishop to target promotion on both ４三 and ８三 seems apparent, but in fact, this falls right into the trap of Demon Killer Opposing Rook.

Figure 2


In response to ▲６五角, Gote jumps the Knight with ▽４五桂, and when Sente defends the ５七 square with ▲４八銀, Gote plays ▽３三角. This is the exact procedure of Demon Killer (Oni-goroshi, 鬼殺し), leading to Figure 2 where the next ▲９九角成 cannot be defended. In this formation, dropping a Bishop to target promotion on both sides often appears, but in most cases, it does not work. Therefore, Sente must proceed carefully with piece development.
