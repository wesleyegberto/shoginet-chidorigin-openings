Horned Silver Central Rook (Tsuno-Gin Nakabisha, ツノ銀中飛車戦法) [1]

Starting with this chapter, we enter the Central Rook (Nakabisha, 中飛車) series. Currently, dynamic powers-style Central Rook lines enjoy tremendous popularity among professional players, to the point that even dedicated Static Rook (居飛車) players regularly adopt them. In Chapter 8, we introduced Primitive Central Rook (原始中飛車) as a classic beginner setup, but from here on, we will explore authentic Central Rook systems all the way up to today's prevailing variations.

To begin, we examine "Tsuno-Gin Nakabisha" (Horned-Silver Central Rook, ツノ銀中飛車), which stood for years as the primary orthodox Central Rook system.

Figure 1 represents the basic formation for Tsuno-Gin Nakabisha facing Sente's Static Rook rapid attack with ▲５七銀左. The name "Tsuno-Gin" (Horned Silver) comes from the Silver on ４三, which resembles the horn of a beetle or the antenna of the Rook. Following this, if Gote plays ▽６四歩 and ▽６三銀, the complete Tsuno-Gin Nakabisha structure is established. If Sente plays ▲４六銀 instead of ▲４六歩, Gote answers with ▽３二金; even if Sente tries ▲３五歩, Gote repels the attack with ▽４五歩. Diagonal Climbing Silver (Naname Bōgin) is ineffective against the ▽３二金 structure.

Similarly, meeting Gote's ▽４四飛 pullback with ▲同角 leads to ▽同角; when Sente plays ▲６六歩, Gote attacks with ▽６五歩, which again yields poor results for Sente. Following the Bishop exchange, Sente pulls back the Climbing Silver to rebuild their formation, but in Figure 2, Gote completes both "horns" with ▽６三銀 and holds two Pawns in hand from the 4th and 5th files, giving Gote a very comfortable game.
