Eiharu-style 19-move Joseki (英春流19手定跡)

Although this departs from Yokofudori for now, as background knowledge for a surprise strategy explained in later chapters—where Gote uses a Yokofudori setup to provoke Sente—we will introduce the "19-Move Joseki" created by former Apprentice Association (the professional training institute) member and top amateur player Eiharu Suzuki.

Moves to Figure 1:

  • ▲７六歩
  • ▽３四歩
  • ▲４八銀
  • ▽８四歩
  • ▲５六歩
  • ▽８五歩
  • ▲５七銀
Figure 1


Moving ▲４八銀 without pushing the Rook's pawn is the strategy known as the "Eiharu Style" (Eiharu-ryu). Even when playing as Gote, the equivalent move would be ▽６二銀. Even when the opponent extends their pawn with ▽８五歩, Sente calmly moves up with ▲５七銀.

Moves from Figure 1 to Figure 2:

  • ▽８六歩
  • ▲同　歩
  • ▽同　飛
  • ▲２二角成
  • ▽同　銀
  • ▲７七角
  • ▽８九飛成
  • ▲２二角成
  • ▽３三角
Figure 2


In response to the Rook-pawn exchange starting with ▽８六歩, Sente plays ▲２二角成 followed by ▲７七角. Driven by momentum, Gote also responds with ▽８九飛成, and meets Sente's ▲２二角成 with ▽３三角. Rooks and Bishops fly fiercely across the board.

Moves from Figure 2 to Figure 3:

  • ▲２一馬
  • ▽９九角成
  • ▲５五桂
Figure 3
　

Instead of exchanging Bishops in response to ▽３三角, Sente plays ▲２一馬, and after ▽９九角成, drops a Knight with ▲５五桂. Although Gote has promoted both their Rook and Bishop and the position might seem like a close match, Sente has a winning advantage (shōsei) at move 19 in Figure 3 with the ▲５五桂 drop. This move threatens ▲６三桂不成, checking the King while aiming at the Silver, forcing Gote to respond—at which point Sente plays ▲１一馬, leaving Gote behind by a Silver.

Of course, there are variations along the way, but including those, this deadly sequence by Sente that entices the opponent's Rook-pawn exchange is called the "19-Move Joseki."

While similar sequences appear in other Shogi openings, the 19-Move Joseki is the most representative example. Keeping this in mind, I would like you to take a look at Gote's surprise tactic from a Yokofudori setup, which will be explained in the next chapter.
