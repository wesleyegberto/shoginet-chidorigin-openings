Against Gote's 4th move ▽３三角, Sente avoids the Bishop exchange with ▲４八銀, and even if Gote plays ▽２二飛 to range their Rook, Sente holds back their Rook Pawn at ２六 to thoroughly neutralize Gote's rapid attack. Even if Gote exchanges Bishops here with ▽８八角成, they have no follow-up rapid attack, so the game proceeds with standard piece development. Since it is difficult for Sente to solidify their King with the Bishop's diagonal remaining open, Sente plays ▲６六歩 to aim for an endurance game.

Against the Static Rook's Left Mino (Hidari Mino, 左美濃) explained in Chapter 52, Gote builds an even more solid Bear-in-the-hole (Anaguma, 穴熊) castle starting with ▽９二香 followed by ▽９一玉. While this Anaguma castle is the strongest defense for the King, its drawback is that it requires many moves to complete. However, since Sente played defensively out of caution against Gote's Move 4 ▽３三角 Opposing Rook, Gote no longer has to worry about an attack being launched before completing the castle.

Figure 3, despite having a different intermediate move order, is a position that appeared in Game 1 of the 62nd Meijin Match. Challengers Moriuchi Ryu-O/Osho employed this tactic as Gote against Meijin/Ouza Habu, starting from a different opening pattern, but likewise advancing ▽３三角 without closing the Bishop's diagonal, and achieved a complete victory from Figure 3. In standard Ranging Rook openings, if one wants to use the Bishop for attacking, an attacking formation like the one in Figure 3 cannot be created without first playing ▽４五歩 with the Pawn blocked at ▽４四歩; as a result, Gote can launch an attack two moves faster.

In Figure 3, even if Sente drops ▲５六歩, Gote plays ▽６六歩 and the attack does not stop. The tactical motif of pushing and sacrificing ▽６五歩 before playing ▽５五銀 is an important key point.

This piece setup is just one example, but since rapid attacks from the Static Rook side are always something the Ranging Rook side must guard against, being able to neutralize them while also taking the initiative to go on the offensive is quite appealing. In that sense, this Opposing Rook Move 4 ▽３三角 can be considered a very powerful strategy.
