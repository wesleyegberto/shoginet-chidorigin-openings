Sente's Attacking Central Rook (先手の攻める中飛車) [1]

In Chapters 58 and 59, during the explanation of Sente's offensive Opposing Rook (向かい飛車) variations, we touched upon the fact that the position can transpose into Central Rook depending on Gote's response. Starting with this chapter, we will examine that specific opening structure.

Moves up to Figure 1

  • ▲７六歩
  • ▽８四歩
  • ▲５六歩
  • ▽５四歩
  • ▲５八飛
Figure 1

If Gote responds to Sente's ▲５六歩 by defending the 5th file early with ▽５四歩 instead of pushing ▽８五歩, swinging the Rook with ▲５八飛 into Central Rook is an effective line. From here, if Gote opens the Bishop's diagonal with ▽３四歩, Sente can exchange Bishops and drop ▲５三角, allowing only Sente to create a Horse. Unlike the Opposing Rook lines, Gote does not have a counter-drop with ▽５三角. Naturally, this raises the question of what happens if Gote plays ▽８五歩 right away—since meeting it with ▲７七角 would block Opposing Rook transitions and narrow Sente's strategic options, giving Gote an edge.

Against ▽８五歩, Sente has the bold option of ignoring it with ▲５五歩. If Gote takes this with ▽同歩, Sente recaptures with ▲同角. If Gote then tries ▽８六歩, Sente responds with ▲同歩, and after ▽同飛, plays ▲７七角—a discovered check that simultaneously attacks the Rook, winning the game for Sente (Figure 3).

Therefore, Gote resorts to the forceful ▽８六歩. However, since Gote cannot allow Sente to create a Promoted Pawn (Tokin) at the head of their King, Gote defends with ▽５二歩. Both sides then prevent the opponent's Rook from invading with ▲７八金 and ▽６二銀. By reaching Figure 2 with Sente floating the Rook via ▲５六飛, Sente has advanced along the 5th file, gained a Pawn, and left Gote without any Pawns in hand, creating a position of distinct advantage for Sente.

Since ▽８五歩 does not yield a favorable result for Gote, Gote instead reinforces the center with ▽６二銀, threatening to open the Bishop's diagonal next with ▽３四歩. Sente pushes forward with ▲５五歩, and in response to Gote's ▽同歩, Sente has two capturing options: ▲同角 and ▲同飛.

First, let us examine the capture with ▲同角. If Gote naively plays ▽８五歩 here, Sente strikes with a double check via ▲３三角成, delivering an instant checkmate and a quick loss for Gote. To prevent this double check, Gote steps aside with ▽４二玉, after which Sente pulls back with ▲７七角. This resulting position grants Sente a full move ahead compared to simply playing ▲７七角 directly earlier. Following Gote's ▽３四歩, Sente strikes back with ▲５五歩 to reach Figure 4. While the balance remains officially even, this ▲５五歩 style Central Rook setup represents a highly potent strategy, which we will analyze further in subsequent chapters.

The variation where Sente recaptures with ▲同飛 instead of ▲同角 will be covered in the next chapter.
