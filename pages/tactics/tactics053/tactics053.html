Sakata-style Opposing Rook (Sakata-ryū Mukaibisha, 阪田流向かい飛車戦法)

In this chapter, we will look at the Sakata-Style Opposing Rook (Sakata-ryū Mukaibisha), a famous strategy said to have been devised by Sakata Sankichi, who is well known through movies and stories.

Figure 1


Starting with a unique opening line where Gote responds to Sente's 3rd move ▲２六歩 with ▽３二金, Gote forms what would normally be considered bad shape by taking ▽同金 when Bishops are exchanged with ▲３三角成, before swinging the Rook to the 2nd file. This strategy is Sakata-ryuu Mukaibisha (Sakata Style Opposing Rook, 阪田流向かい飛車). If Sente does not exchange Bishops, it becomes a different opening, so it can be described as a mutually agreed-upon game. ▲３八銀 is a move played in anticipation of Sakata Style to prepare for the 2nd file.

This Sakata-ryu is an all-out strategy that is 90% attack, taking the offensive while remaining in Igyoku (Sitting King, 居玉). By playing ▽２四歩 ▲同歩 ▽同金, the Gold on 3三 from the Bishop exchange is utilized to counterattack along the Rook's file. This is the main aim of this strategy.

Sente's ▲５六角 aims both at ▲３四角 (if ▽同金, then ▲２二飛成) and ▲８三角成. Since there is no move that defends against both simultaneously, Gote plays ▽３三銀 to allow the Bishop to promote, followed by ▽２五金. Below, in Figure 3, the next ▽２七歩成 cannot be defended, resulting in a winning position for Gote.

This Sakata-style can be said to be quite an interesting strategy as a off-book play (Rikisen, 力戦) Opposing Rook.
