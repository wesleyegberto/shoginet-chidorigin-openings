No-Guard Strategy (Nō-gādo Senpō, ノーガード戦法) [1]

During the Yokofudori setup sequences explained so far, Gote has a surprise strategy called the "No-Guard Strategy" (Nō-gādo Senpō) designed to provoke Sente. We will introduce this tactic and examine the countermeasures available to Sente.

In a Yokofudori setup, when Sente plays ▲７八金, Gote refrains from playing ▽３二金 and instead suddenly initiates a Rook-pawn exchange with ▽８六歩. Because Gote charges forward without protecting the head of their Bishop, this surprise tactic is called the "No-Guard Strategy." Naturally, faced with this provocative move, Sente is tempted to strike back with ▲２四歩.

Allowing Sente to push ▲２四歩 is the aim of the No-Guard Strategy, leading to Figure 2 through a sequence similar to the 19-Move Joseki in the previous chapter. From here, even if Gote plays ▽８九馬 followed by Sente's ▲１一角成, unlike the 19-Move Joseki, Gote lacks the ▽５五桂 counter, leaving Sente with a playable position.

Furthermore, even if Gote plays ▽同馬 in Figure 2, Sente responds with ▲同桂, resulting in a position that can still be considered easier for Sente to play—though it is admittedly quite a frightening variation. In the sequence, if Gote cuts with the Bishop first via ▽８八角成 instead of taking with the Rook via ▽８八飛成 after Sente's ▲２一飛成, Sente plays ▲同金, and after ▽同飛成, drops ▲３三角 for a check-and-Dragon fork, bringing the game to an end right there.

As mentioned in Chapter 40 Double Side Pawn Picker (Ai-Yokofudori), throwing off the opponent's preparation is also an effective tactic. Thus, the question becomes whether there is a move that avoids falling for Gote's provocation while still punishing the No-Guard Strategy; here, I recommend quietly defending with ▲８七歩. You might think "Is that all?", but this is not merely a timid defensive move.

When Sente defends with ▲８七歩 in Figure 1, Gote has two choices for retreating their Rook: ▽８四飛 and ▽８二飛. First, if Gote retreats to ▽８四飛, Sente builds the attacking formation from the Double Wing Attack (Aigakari) covered in Chapter 29. In fact, Gote's ▽８四飛 shape is actually dangerous, setting the stage for Sente's attack to explode from here.
