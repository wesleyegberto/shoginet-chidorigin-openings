In the previous chapter, we covered the variation where Sente captured with ▲同角 after ▲５五歩 ▽同歩, but this time we will look at capturing with ▲同飛 with check. Here, Gote determines the state of the 8th file early with ▽８五歩 ▲７七角. Since Sente is forced to respond with ▲７七角, this is the optimal timing. After ▽３二玉, Sente solidifies the left flank with ▲７八金; the moment Gote opens the Bishop's diagonal with ▽３四歩, Sente plays ▲５四飛. Rather than a mere retreat, this move actively targets Gote's Pawn on ３四. However, Gote exchanges Bishops and drops ▽４五角—a brilliant move that simultaneously protects the ３四 Pawn, attacks Sente's Rook, and aims for ▽２七角成. While Figure 3 appears to be a failure for Sente, it is actually a setup into which Gote has fallen directly.

Sente dodges the attack with ▲５五飛, counter-attacking Gote's Bishop. After Gote promotes with ▽２七角成, Sente immediately swings to ▲２五飛, forcing Gote's Horse back to ▽５四馬 as the sole legal answer. A forced sequence of unavoidable moves follows, arriving at Figure 4 with Sente's Pawn drop ▲２四歩—a position where Sente is already overwhelmingly winning (Shōsei).

You might wonder how this could be, but from this point forward, no matter how Gote tries to vary their response, Sente's victory is absolute. We will verify the details of this in the next chapter.
