Sente's Aggressive Opposing Rook (先手の攻める向かい飛車) [2]

In response to Sente playing ▲８八飛 to form a Opposing Rook (Mukaibisha, 向かい飛車), Gote played ▽６二銀 in the previous chapter. In this chapter, we will explain the variations when Gote plays ▽３四歩 instead, opening the Bishop's diagonal with wild battle in mind.

Against Gote's ▽３四歩, Sente has several options. Among them, the most aggressive line is playing ▲２二角成　▽同銀　▲５三角 to create a Promoted Bishop (Horse), but Gote responds in kind with ▽５七角, plunging into a wild battle. Naturally, this does not mean the position becomes bad for Sente, and Sente has interesting plans available, but it strays far from the essence of Opposing Rook. Furthermore, Sente would want to sidestep Gote's intention, as Gote played ▽３四歩 precisely looking for a wild battle.

Alternatively, Sente could choose the Masuda Style Mukaibisha (升田流向かい飛車), playing ▲６八銀 to maintain the open Bishop's diagonal and build an offensive formation. However, since this overlaps significantly with the Move 4 ▽３三角 style explained in Chapter 57, we will close the Bishop's diagonal with ▲６六歩 this time. Gote's ▽７四歩 prepares ▽７三桂 or ▽７三銀 against Sente's counterattacking ▲８六歩. It also serves to prevent Sente from taking the vanguard pawn with ▲７五歩—an effective strategy that will be explained in a later chapter. Regarding ▽６四歩, as explained in Chapter 50, if Gote plays ▽４二玉 without pushing this Pawn, Sente immediately plays ▲８六歩, leading to a disadvantage for Gote due to the line where Gote's Rook is captured directly.

▲６五歩 is Sente's intended move. If Gote captures with ▽同歩, Sente plays ▲２二角成 for a Bishop exchange followed by ▲６四角, executing a double attack (Oote-Hisha) targeting both King and Rook. Also, if Gote plays ▽４四歩, Sente captures with ▲６四歩, leaving Gote at a disadvantage, so Gote responds with ▽５三銀. After exchanging Bishops and playing ▲６三角, Gote counters with ▽４四角; if Sente defends with ▲７三銀 or ▲７三桂, Gote aims to play ▽６五銀 to slide the Silver in with ▽７六銀, or trap and kill the Bishop with ▽６二歩. In Figure 2, playing ▲６六歩 is a clever move. This tactical motif of dropping a Pawn directly in the line of fire of a Rook, Bishop, or Lance to interpose is called Chūai no Fudai (Interposing Pawn Drop, 中合いの歩). Forcing ▽同角 and then being able to defend with ▲７七銀 with the tempo of attacking the Bishop is the true effect of this interposing drop. Against ▽５七角成, Sente responds with ▲５四角成 targeting the Silver. Retreating here with ▽５三銀 allows Sente's ▲５五馬—a double threat attacking both Rook and Silver—so Gote plays ▽７三銀 instead. Sente then plays ▲５八飛 to strongly force a exchange; if Gote plays ▽同馬, Sente responds with ▲同金右, leaving no opening for Gote to drop a Rook into Sente's camp.
